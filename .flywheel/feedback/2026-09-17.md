## What worked

- **`fh agents` as a shape oracle beat authoring from taste.** Deriving the canonical AGENTS.md
  skeleton (`content_id=095decf2…`, 64 sections over 117/221 mirror repos) meant the section set
  was measured, not argued. The `.fh-agents.toml` synonym map then made every ABSENT row either
  real or explicitly argued — zero UNMEASURED.
- **Wrapping house gates instead of re-implementing them.** `50-house-gates.sh` delegates to
  `dag-validate-gate.sh`, `close-evidence-gate.sh`, `commit-evidence-lint.sh` and inherits their
  proven `--selftest` arms. Three gates wired in one pass; nothing to maintain.
- **The guards caught me, repeatedly, and were right every time.** `dcg` denied `git add -A` in a
  shared worktree; the `commit-msg` hook refused my first commit for having no verification level.
  Both are the kind of rule an agent "knows" and forgets under momentum.
- **Manifest-first vendoring paid for itself on the first run.** `sync-docs.sh` reported the local
  ripwire checkout 1502 commits behind while the installed binary was a *third* revision. Nobody
  would have noticed that by reading.
- **Asking "anything not done?" was worth more than any single fix.** It produced the two oracles
  I had skipped (`stamp-check`, `fh oracles`) and moved the score 32→47 PASS.

## Friction

- **Four of my findings this session were instrument errors, not defects.** `^PASS` grepped against
  `name PASS` rows ("empty scan set"); `ripwire` matched inside `*-skill-tripwire.sh` ("a hook was
  wired"); a usage banner read as a removed `--doctor`; a fixture's own hooks read as the gate
  misfiring. Every one cost a round trip. **The pattern: I trusted my reader before I trusted the
  tool.** All four are now rows in `NEGATIVE_EVIDENCE.md`.
- **`init.templateDir = ~/.git-templates` silently breaks every tool that builds a throwaway git
  fixture.** `commit-evidence-lint.sh --selftest` fails at "fixture repo setup" and PASSES with
  `GIT_CONFIG_GLOBAL=/dev/null`. This is machine-wide and will hit any future gate with that shape
  (bead `jev-foundry-selftest-git-template-coc`).
- **`stamp-check` scores vendored clones as first-party.** In a lane whose root is ~20 other
  people's repos, `p5-autofix` counts 969 upstream files, `rc0-declared-path` claims a vendored
  Rust crate as our CLI, and `p10-commit-sweep` flagged a literal `TODO` inside a *recorded
  transcript fixture*. Three false positives that cost real triage (bead
  `jev-stampcheck-vendored-false-positives-lfn`).
- **`fh standards stamp` is unreachable from here.** It needs an authoritative five-column target
  manifest that this lane cannot author without committing the exact SUBSTRATE anti-pattern fh
  forbids (bead `jev-route-standards-manifest-6yq`).
- **Three agents in one worktree with no cross-session channel.** `hub list` shows only my own
  subagents; the sibling panes are separate omp sessions. When their gate went RED mid-edit I could
  not tell them — the only channel was a commit message they may never read.

## Workflow-improvement suggestions

1. **Add an "instrument-first" rule to the honesty skills:** before reporting an empty/zero/absent
   result, re-run the reader against a known-positive control. Three of my four retractions would
   have died in 10 seconds. This belongs in `just-say-no-to-process-porn` or a new
   `verify-the-reader` clause, not in this lane's AGENTS.md where only I will see it.
2. **Fix `init.templateDir` at the source, or make the template hook fixture-aware.** Either
   isolate git config in every loop-kit selftest, or have `commit-msg` no-op when
   `GIT_DIR` is under `$TMPDIR`. The second is one line and fixes the whole class.
3. **`stamp-check` should honour the target repo's ignore rules** when enumerating a code surface,
   or support the `DELIBERATELY_NOT` allowance row the manifest already describes. An
   evaluation-lane archetype (a root full of vendored clones) is going to recur.
4. **Give panes in one NTM session a shared channel.** A file-backed inbox under `.ntm/` that
   `hub` can read across sessions would have let me hand the sibling their RED gate immediately.
5. **`fh agents --stamp` should offer a dry-run that shows the rename plan without a repo.**
   I left `[headings]` empty because the plan is only visible after committing to a target.
