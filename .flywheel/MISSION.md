# Jev mission

Typed classifiers that earn their place in our tools: each one proven live, measured, and delivered through one model-neutral CLI (`classifier`) that makes the next one cheap.

Authority: ROADMAP.md Mission, approved by Joshua on 2026-10-04 ("yes i like c"). ROADMAP.md remains the plan of record; .omp/mission.toml is its machine form.

Six pillars: Live, Measured, Honest, Local-first, Portable, Current. A feature earns completion only through its named acceptance, fresh both-ways runtime proof where required, measured value/cost, independent verification, and a commit on origin/main. Offline plumbing is not live backend proof. Local routing requires the preregistered non-inferiority receipt; otherwise retain Jev or the rule incumbent.

Shared-tooling failures go uphill to omp-kit, not a local substitute. Fleet-watch remains off during the watcher-free restart. Workers implement the existing classifier DAG; the director independently integrates their callbacks before assigning more work.
