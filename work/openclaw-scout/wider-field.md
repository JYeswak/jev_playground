# Wider-field scan: decision models and evaluation practice

Scope: source inspection only. Revisions are pinned below; no model calls or quality claims were made.

## Findings

| Source | Source-backed mechanism | Adoption decision |
|---|---|---|
| Cloudflare Clef / Clef-flash | Cloudflare's 2026-10-01 launch describes System-One-compatible Noul, Choice, and Score answers with per-option probabilities; it says up to 64 questions can share a request. The Clef-flash model page documents a 65,536-token context and text/JSON/image/video input. Cloudflare reports 43 benchmark runs and claims 13x lower median latency for Clef-flash than Jev; these are vendor-reported results, not an independent comparison. | Candidate provider/model only. Do not substitute it for Jev or infer quality from the launch table. There is no measured local consumer or same-invocation held-out comparison here; no bead. |
| Jev Decision Index | The pinned Space describes a frozen edition, an all-40-results inclusion rule, equal-weight benchmark areas, and a chance-corrected `balanced_skill` headline; unrun interactive environments are excluded rather than imputed. Its README says the static bundle is generated from committed run artifacts and builder outputs. The pinned `check.js` inspects the News-tab categories/items and trending calculation, not leaderboard recomputation. This is a community-maintained reproduction tracker, not a general workflow-safety oracle. | Reuse only the completeness and frozen-edition discipline when reporting a leaderboard. Our candidate work already freezes inputs and refuses incomplete cohorts; the leaderboard metric does not match our operational-outcome contract. No bead.
| `kenhuangus/jev-usecases` | `coding_agent_guardrails.py::gate_tool_call` applies deterministic protected-path denial before the model call, then requires every named safety proposition to meet an affirmative floor; uncertain/high-risk calls go to confirmation. `tests/test_decision_safety.py` exercises each safety predicate at block/ask/allow boundaries and checks both inbound and outbound guarded-answer gates. README explicitly says fixture success proves typed-call behavior, not policy correctness or unattended-production readiness. | Good test pattern, not a demonstrated local gap. Our Jev command-risk gate already uses a frozen five-Noul bundle and strict `maxScore > CUT` (`.omp/jev-kit/gate.ts:51-68`); tests cover below/equal-cut behavior (`.omp/hooks/post/jev-gate-observe.test.mjs:56-64`). No defect-backed bead for adding a second policy shape.
| TypeSafe WorkflowEvals | The pinned `core/contract.py` binds `Call.state_hash` and `questions_hash` to canonical payload fingerprints, records `CaseResult.input_status` and case hashes, forbids invalid result shapes, and carries a schema version. `core/runner.py::run_configuration` hashes data, policy, workflow, dependency versions, and lockfile into a manifest; resume rejects a changed manifest or stale case checkpoint. README says the resolved dataset revision is recorded and failed cases remain in outputs. The README also states that the eval assumes workflow code is correct and measures agreement with its chosen references. | Strong reproducibility pattern, already present in our experimental runners: `work/jev-pgtu/adapter_runner.py:482-510` verifies data/source hashes and join integrity; `work/jev-uhc5/run.mjs:159-161,693-696` binds source hashes to preflight. The local row-provenance check also caught the mislabeled JSON-array/JSONL artifact. No additive consumer defect; no bead. |

## Decision

No new adoption bead filed. The mechanisms with a concrete local consumer already have corresponding frozen-input, hash-binding, complete-cohort, and safe-action patterns. The remaining proposals either lack a measured consumer (Clef as an alternate) or would replace our operational outcome with a leaderboard metric. Reopen only with a named consumer and a measured defect or an independently scored held-out result.
