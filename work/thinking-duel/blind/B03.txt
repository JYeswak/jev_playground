ITEM B03 | PROMPT p11
RUBRIC-CORE: SEO crawl index Core-Web-Vitals redirects
---ANSWER---
After a redesign, start at Layer 1: crawlability → indexation, per dependency chain — uncrawlable pages make content fixes worthless.

Check `robots.txt` for unintentional blocks, XML sitemap for stale URLs, Search Console index coverage vs expected count, redirect chains/loops from old URLs, and canonical tags pointing correctly.
