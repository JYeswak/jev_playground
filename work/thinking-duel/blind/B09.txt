ITEM B09 | PROMPT p01
RUBRIC-CORE: rate-limit throttle per-user quota
---ANSWER---
Enforce per-user/per-API-key limits as primary, with a lower per-endpoint limit on expensive search — not just global or per-IP.

Use sliding-window counter (or token bucket for controlled bursts) in centralized Redis with atomic check+increment, TTL matched to window.

Return `429` with `Retry-After` plus `X-RateLimit-Limit/Remaining/Reset` headers, and count all requests regardless of status.

Add global safety-net limit plus queuing/cached degradation and sustained-429 alerting to catch the next hog.
