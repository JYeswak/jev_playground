ITEM B07 | PROMPT p05
RUBRIC-CORE: triage priority SLA urgent-queue
---ANSWER---
Don't use `jev mail` here — it's for a personal backlog, not a shared queue.

Use `jev triage`: it classifies each message as it lands by how soon, what kind, is a person needed, and is the sender blocked — routing to now / today / queue / ignore.

Urgent refund cases score `route: now` with deadline + frustrated flags and surface ahead of password resets; pass `--customer-domain` so customer mail outranks internal noise.
