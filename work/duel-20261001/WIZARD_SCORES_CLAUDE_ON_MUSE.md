## Verdict

Muse's listed top five are not my top five. The strongest actionable idea is **#17, an independently labeled audit of Nimble-cleared gate commands**; it was omitted from Muse's top five despite a recent planted dangerous command that the local cascade cleared. **#6, the semantic skill-hint retry, merits one tightly bounded experiment** because it is the exact new-design condition allowed by R133, not another lexical variation. #16 and #15 have plausible evidence-led follow-ups. The universal cascade (#30) is materially overclaimed: a single gate-screen result does not generalize to native judge calls, and current local-vs-Jev comparisons show task-specific disagreement.

These are judgment scores, not measured product outcomes. I weighted observed need, practical benefit, correctness risk, implementation fit, and ongoing complexity. A 0–1000 score is not a probability. No idea earns a 900+ no-brainer score.

## Evidence corrections that change the ranking

- **Cascade evidence is narrow and not yet a safety win.** In the latest cited one-hour slice, 102 rows used the cascade: 98 cleared locally and 4 reached paid Jev; 55 additional rows were screen failures. The 96% figure describes the paid-call share of that admitted cascade subset, not every Jev call or every command. A planted dangerous `rm-rf-nonexistent` case scored 0.02 locally and bypassed paid review; on a different routed dangerous plant, paid Jev also returned `flag:false`. A Jev-only audit is therefore not ground truth. See `EVAL.md` §§jev-3m65, jev-nr3c, and 2026-10-01 docs-sync; `work/jev-inventory/expected.json` §gate-cascade.
- **Local answers are not interchangeable with native paid answers.** On 300 real text-only stops, Nimble flagged 110 versus Jev's 8 (kappa 0.073); on 200 auto-thinking prompts, exact agreement was 0.66 and weighted kappa 0.46, with Nimble p50 562 ms versus TypeSafe 351 ms. Those surfaces stayed on TypeSafe. A broad router needs per-surface evidence and an interceptable seam, neither implied by the gate cascade. See `EVAL.md` §2026-10-01 native Jev turned on fleet-wide.
- **The volume multiplier is wrong.** The source census reports 17,521 judge calls total across the reported period and 15,448 from 2026-09-24 through 2026-09-30 (about 2.2k/day), not 16k/day. The proposal's “16k/day” upside is unsupported; the AGENTS estimate is about $3/week for roughly 16k calls, not 16k calls each day. See `EVAL.md` §4970 scoreboard and `AGENTS.md` §Local vs paid.
- **The retired-rule loader premise is stale.** The 125 claim-rule firings came from sessions that had already loaded the rule. The follow-up census found zero firings in sessions started after retirement; the observed boundary is stale in-memory registration until session end, not a fresh-session loader defect. A loader guard cannot unload already-running sessions. See `EVAL.md` §jev-pn7b and `NEGATIVE_EVIDENCE.md` R132/R133.
- **Skill-hint retry has a real, narrow acceptance condition.** R133 permits a new semantic-matching design only if it reaches at least 25% hints and zero misroutes on a per-row dev set committed before live calls. N2 recorded 0 hints in 48 rows; that is safe silence, not evidence of utility. The semantic approach has not been measured. See `NEGATIVE_EVIDENCE.md` R133 and `EVAL.md` §jev-4nyy N2.
- **Several listed seams are already demoted.** Smart stop is IDLE-BY-DESIGN (76 organic checks, 0 continuations); web-search rerank is OFF/NO-BENEFIT (0 opens in its measured rows, with a caveat that untracked tools may hide opens); skill hint is OFF; bash-risk screening is NO-SIGNAL. These are not automatic reasons never to revisit, but a new proposal needs new evidence. See `work/jev-inventory/expected.json` and `EVAL.md` §§jev-3m65, jev-ib1h, and 2026-10-01 skill-hint results.

## My top five

1. **#17 — 790/1000.** Best because it tests a current safety assumption before expanding the cascade. Require blinded independent labels, stratification by command class, a predeclared confidence bound, and an explicit privacy boundary. Jev disagreement is informative but cannot be the sole truth oracle.
2. **#6 — 660/1000.** Best new design opportunity under R133. Commit the per-row dev/negative set before any live call; require both ≥25% useful hints and zero misroutes. A pass is evidence for that set and model, not a broad reliability guarantee.
3. **#16 — 640/1000.** Worth a small keyless audit of whether soft-kept memories add task-relevant information. Do not tune toward token reduction alone; a lower token count with lost relevant context is a regression.
4. **#15 — 560/1000.** Evaluate per-class cascade policy only after #17 supplies independent labels. A threshold cannot repair a confident-low false negative; define which classes must always pay or use deterministic guards.
5. **#30 — 500/1000.** The cost-saving motive is legitimate, but the universal form fails feasibility and evidence transfer. Keep it as a research direction, not a build recommendation. The narrower current gate cascade must first resolve its screen failures and false-clear risk.

**Overall:** prioritize evidence about the existing cascade's false-negative rate before multiplying it. Run the semantic hint test only against R133's exact preregistered bar. Everything else stays exploratory or parked until a named consumer and observed defect are stronger than the current evidence. No live Jev calls, implementation, tests, bead mutation, or commit were part of this scoring pass.
