# Ten areas where Jev and Clef typed classifiers have the most impact, ranked by evidence

Research date: 2026-10-03. The repository was read only. No live Jev or Clef calls were made.

**Citation conventions**
- Paths are relative to `/Users/josh/Developer/jev`.
- **NE** = `NEGATIVE_EVIDENCE.md`.
- **BJ** = `.beads/issues.jsonl`. The line number is the bead row.
- **CS** = conductor scoreboard 2026-10-04 (`surface-census.py --scoreboard --days 7`).
- **DOC/** = `docs-mirror/typesafe/`.
- **Recompute** = sub-agent offline runs of `python3 work/local-decision-arms/run.py --score|--platt clefflash` and `vendor.py --score|--platt clefflash` this session. These were keyless, cost $0, read only committed rows, and left `git status` clean.
- `[DERIVED]` = my arithmetic on cited numbers.
- `[INFERENCE]` = my judgement, not a measurement.

**Ranking rule `[INFERENCE]`.** Areas are sorted by evidence tier first:
- A: win on our own real or organic rows against a same-row incumbent.
- B: win on a public or held-out benchmark.
- C: mechanism or usage counts only.

Within a tier, areas are sorted by exposure (7-day volume from CS or census), then by the cost of an error. The rank is a judgement over the cited rows, not a computed metric.

**Shared unit costs**
- Jev `jev-1.13.0` costs $0.042 per million input tokens; output is free (DOC/models.md:13,18).
- Rate limit is 1,200 requests/min (DOC/models.md:14,19).
- Measured cost for 1,000 short-state answers: $0.0158 (Score), $0.0162 (Choice), $0.0287 (Noul) (docs/LEDGER.md:166).
- Clef-flash has $0 marginal spend. It takes about 3 s/call on this Mac (README.md:132). Recompute medians were 4,388 ms on Banking77 and 2,834 ms on vendored code.
