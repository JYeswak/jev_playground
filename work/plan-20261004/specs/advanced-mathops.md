# Advanced math ops: audit, techniques, ranked upgrades (classifier CLI)

Scope: read-only audit of the math the repo already uses, a map from 15 skills to classifier-CLI uses, and outside techniques. No live Jev/Clef calls were made. "Recomputed" means I re-ran arithmetic over committed rows or local logs in this session; the snippets were not committed. Appendix R lists the inputs and formulas so anyone can re-run them.

Headline findings, all recomputed from data already in the repo:

1. **Clef+Platt does not beat Jev on calibration once both get the same treatment.** The Banking77 bar compared Clef with Platt to Jev without it (`work/local-decision-arms/run.py:194`, `BAR-b77-platt.md:11-12`). I fit the same Platt map on Jev's own 200 dev rows. Jev's ECE drops from .1017 to .0276; Clef+Platt is .0246. The 95% paired bootstrap CI on the difference is [-.010, +.037]. Both values are at or below the ECE a perfectly calibrated model would show at n=600 (mean .032 for Jev's score distribution). **The accuracy gap is real:** .962 vs .787, McNemar 107/2, p≈2e-29.
2. **"Clef beats Jev on vendored code" is not supported.** Held AUC is .839 vs .827, DeLong z=.42, p=.67. With equal Platt treatment Jev is better calibrated: ECE .072 vs .081.
3. **The memory filter's drop precision of .985 (67/68) does not separate from the always-drop constant.** The constant scores .94 on that sample, and P(X≥67 | p=.94, n=68) = .080. The quantity that matters is relevant-drop: 1 of 6 relevant items was dropped (Wilson [.03, .56]).
4. **The vendor cut 0.35 was fitted by Youden J on a 60/60 balanced dev set.** At 5% deployment prevalence its predicted precision is .21 (95% interval [.14, .33]). That matches the organic keep-side precision of .19-.24 that sank the earlier designs.
5. **The `filterBudgetMs` model does not explain what the live log shows.** Since 2026-10-03, 1,328 of 2,921 scored-or-deadline items (45.5%) ended `deadline-keep`, and 137 of 409 turns scored nothing. Over the same rows, scored-call p95 is 326 ms.

## Appendix R: how the numbers were recomputed (no live calls)

- **Banking77 splits:** reproduced from `run.py:24-26`: `SAMPLE = sorted(Random(7).sample(range(3080), 600))`, `DEV = sorted(Random(8).sample(rest, 200))`. Jev rows: `work/choice-banking77/rows-full-jev.jsonl` filtered to SAMPLE and DEV, label mapping as in `run.py:28,115`. Clef rows: `rows-clefflash{,-dev}.jsonl`.
- **Platt fits:** GD as in `run.py:175-183`, checked against damped Newton. ECE as in `run.py:83-94`.
- **Bootstrap:** 2,000 paired resamples of the 600 held ids.
- **Noise floor:** 500 draws of y~Bernoulli(p) over each arm's calibrated p.
- **Vendor:** labels from `work/vendor-paste/sample.json` (dev 120 = 60 pos, held 200 = 100 pos). Jev from `vendor-rows.jsonl` (`split`), Clef from `vendor-rows-clefflash{,-dev}.jsonl`.
- **DeLong:** structural components V10/V01 with paired covariance, two-sided normal p.
- **Stacking:** 2-feature logistic regression (Newton, L2 1e-3) on dev logits.
- **Cascade:** τ is the smallest dev share with dev accuracy ≥ Clef dev .95 − .01 (τ=.75), applied unchanged to held.
- **Conformal (T7):** LAC score 1 − p_true on DEV, q̂ = ⌈(n+1)(1−α)⌉-th order statistic.
- **Memory log:** statuses and latencies from `~/.local/state/jev/memory-filter.jsonl` (32,017 rows; turn = (instance, promptHash); window ts ≥ 2026-10-03). Max-of-4: 20,000 bootstrap draws from 4,950 scored latencies. Budget change commit `0ea5e91f` (2026-10-02 01:00 −0600).
- **Wilson/PPV:** z = 1.959964; PPV = s·π/(s·π + f·(1−π)); interval from a Beta(65,37) × Beta(13,89) posterior over s and f.
