APPLY B5 AND THE REGIME FALLS OUT EXACTLY:
  Bit 1 in-distribution: TF-IDF IS the lexical baseline (word frequency) and it scores 98.4%. YES -> NO SEAT.
  Bit 1 out-of-distribution: the same lexical baseline collapses to 70-73%. NO -> SEAT.
THE SEAT IS NOT 'SPAM'. THE SEAT IS DISTRIBUTION SHIFT AND COLD START - any task where you cannot obtain labels that resemble what you will actually see. That is a large real category (new abuse campaigns, novel phishing, emerging failure modes, day-one classification) and it is precisely the regime none of our five surfaces were in.

THIS REFRAMES OUR OWN RESULT RATHER THAN OVERTURNING IT. Every one of our five tasks had a STABLE distribution and an available rule, so the cheap thing won and we were right to ship it. We measured the one regime where Jev cannot win and generalised from it.
