## H1 — 885: loudest pain in the duel, narrowest remaining gap

**Pain VERIFIED at the highest level available.** Codex #36718 ("Add
evidence-backed completion reports", open, 2026-08-03, 4 substantive
comments) is not a complaint but a *product spec written by users*: per-
requirement status entries, validation commands with actual exit state,
focused-vs-full-suite distinction, stale-evidence rules, compaction
persistence, machine-readable output. Commenters extend it to
event-sourced freshness-aware records and evidence-invalidation on later
mutation. This single thread validates H1's demand more than any
benchmark could.

**Incumbent gap NARROWER than presented — the −55.** `backcheck`
(VectorInstitute, Rust, Apache-2.0) deterministically verifies done-
claims against session transcripts with zero LLM calls, including the
stale-green shape (files changed after green run) and suite-weakening
detection, Stop-hook install, and machine-readable output. `evigate`
(MIT, TypeScript) adds proven/contradicted/unknown verdicts with reason
codes, an 8-operator mutation harness at 113/113 with a documented
anti-circularity redesign, dual-layer redaction, and an honest
limitations section. Both are nascent (0–1 stars) but real, maintained,
and directly on H1's claimed ground. The file positions them as "partial
substitutes to benchmark against"; the accurate position is that the
deterministic evidence-verification core already ships twice, and H1's
surviving wedge is (a) cross-harness portability (both incumbents are
Claude-shaped) and (b) Jev-calibrated adjudication where deterministic
detectors abstain to `unknown`. That wedge is real but smaller than the
file's framing, and 940 prices the whole demo, not the wedge.

**Not generation in costume.** Explicit deterministic-checks-first with
Jev adjudicating claim-evidence relation only. Clean.

## Rank file — 800: transparent, hedged, two material flaws

Credit first: the ClaimLint/backcheck finds are genuine research value
(backcheck verified real above — pane 2 found the single most important
adjacent artifact in the duel and cited it against its own side's
interest in H1's section). Hedging is consistent ("demand signals, not
proof", "supports price-audit demand, not necessarily this CLI's
adoption"). The NO-CLAIM is complete.

**Flaw 1 — asymmetric evidentiary standards.** Demo-9's demand rests on
vendor adoption reporting and comparison marketing (accepted as voice);
demo-4's 480 rests on "no external user voices that exact close-path
need" — while Codex #36718 *is* an external user voicing essentially
that need (evidence-backed completion reports with exit codes and stale
rules), linked in pane 2's own hunt file. Vendor material counts for
the pick; a user-authored product spec doesn't count for the reject.
That asymmetry decides the file's top and its largest anti-consensus
call, so it costs real points.

**Flaw 2 — category error on demo-4.** CodeRabbit/Qodo/Gre review
*code*; the false-completion problem is about *task state* (was the
acceptance satisfied, were the checks run). A team can own every review
bot on the market and still ship an agent's confident "done" over
unrun verification — different seams, different buyers within the team.
"Already served by PR review" does not survive contact with the
mechanism. −60 combined for the two flaws, both localized to demo-9 up
/ demo-4 down; the 360–540 band (demo-3/6/1/7) and the demo-8 kill read
fair and I register no dissent there.
