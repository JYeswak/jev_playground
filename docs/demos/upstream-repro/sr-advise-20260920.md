Trigger B (OURS, one link): sr never descends symlinked dirs (`discovery.rs:477-494`,

**HIS half (upstream finding, worth an issue):** the withhold is all-or-nothing by design
("Root-level failures… still withhold authority globally", `resolution.rs:525-529`), which
makes `sr` unusable on any real-world Claude store: symlinked skill dirs are the ecosystem's
normal sharing mechanism (5 of our ripwire-* skills link into
`/Users/josh/.local/share/ripwire/skills/`), and a 10k-entry cap is small for a 651-skill
store. One skipped symlink → zero advice is a hair trigger, not a calibration.

**HIS half #2 (second upstream finding): `malformed-metadata` false-positives on Claude's own
`allowed-tools` convention.** `src/roster/frontmatter.rs:354-363`: any frontmatter line
containing `" *"` (space-star) is rejected as `AliasForbidden`. `Bash(socraticode *)`,
`Bash(jsm *)`, `Bash(bash *)` — the standard glob form Claude writes — trip it. Confirmed by
single-record imports: socraticode/jsm/skill-builder fail `Metadata`, while research-scout
(`Bash(./scripts/*)` — no space-star in frontmatter) passes. The guard cannot distinguish a
YAML alias (`*name` as a node) from a glob inside a plain scalar. Real-YAML parsers accept
these files.

**`unsupported-layout` (167) is OURS and harmless:** `claude_invocation`
(`resolution.rs:404-415`) accepts only `<name>/SKILL.md` at depth 2. Our store litters other
`.md` at skill top level (`dcg/SELF-TEST.md`), root-level files
(`RESEARCH-aaas-synthesis.md`), and deeper trees (`references/`, `scripts/` docs). Per design
these withhold nothing (`resolution.rs:458-463`, "cannot shadow a supported binding").
