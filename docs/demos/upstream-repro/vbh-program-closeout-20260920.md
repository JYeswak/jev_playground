## The four findings a reader should take

**1. A named subset overturned the aggregate every single time.** Three for three:
`drop-largest` looked best on savings and lost a third of substantive reuse; `eww`'s
DISCRIMINATES was carried entirely by clear cases; `vbh.4`'s +15 lift was entirely easy-case with
**all five errors in one trap class**. *Pooled numbers were wrong in the optimistic direction
every time, and the subset caught it every time.*

**2. Refuse against the favourable number, or the bar means nothing.** `.4` cleared the bar its
dispatch set — beat always-abstain **and** argmax — and still refused, because
*the abstention band caught the cautious middle, not the hard class*, and nothing distinguishes a
hard turn *ex ante*. A router that acts would fire hardest where the judge is worst.

**3. Search before inventing, and the search pays immediately.** `.3` was about to author a skill
we already owned; a five-second `jsm search` found `evaluation-framework` and cut the bead to the
three mechanisms genuinely absent — outcome join, co-presence, random-judge calibration. The same
check found the skill was **installed but untracked**, a state none of our audits modelled.

**4. Fixing a defect one level down does not kill it.** Mention-vs-use moved from shell quoting
into program literals when the first fix landed, and `.1` had to chase it there. Twenty instances
this session across a model, a regex, an observer, a placeholder hunt, and fourteen of our own
measurement selectors.
