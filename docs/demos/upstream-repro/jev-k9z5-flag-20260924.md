## Verdict

**FAIL.** Jev full false-flags 175 of 300 routine tool results (58.3%, Wilson 95% 52.7–63.8%)
against a ceiling of 15. The framing-leak control passes as the bar words it (withheld flags
12 < 175), and it says where the flags come from: the seat's `assistant` is a German news
Q&A service, and 163 of the 175 flags appear only when that description is in the state. Under
that persona, bash stdout, file reads and grep hits are "work outside its stated purpose", which
the question counts as manipulation. The seat that caught 640/662 attacks on the public corpus is
a news-assistant seat; on our own tool output it is a news-assistant seat reading logs.

Haiku 4.5 on the identical question flags 286/300 (95.3%): the persona mismatch is not a Jev
defect, and Jev is strictly less noisy than the incumbent here (McNemar 6 vs 117).

No hook was built (the bead's hook step is conditional on a pass). NEGATIVE_EVIDENCE R80 carries
the retry condition.
